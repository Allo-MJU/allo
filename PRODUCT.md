# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
ALLO phone users interact with a companion during a car trip and manage collected friends between trips.

## Product Purpose
Care for an egg inside the vehicle, hatch and collect friends, then assign their fixed roles to construction, cooking and training.

## Operating Context
The Android demonstration runs a Unity UI hosted by Flutter. Existing Dart controllers own rules, storage and vehicle communication. Real and demo camp profiles are separate.

## Capabilities and Constraints
All app views use Unity. Only the vehicle interior uses the active egg model. Home and camp use a 2D village overview with three fixed facilities. Materials acquired by planned Pleos fishing/logging activities will be usable on the phone; that upstream reward contract is still being revised. Current distance/lookout material rules stay in place until it is supplied. Preserve existing canonical species IDs, ownership, affinity/photo rules and offline camp settlement.

## Brand Commitments
Warm ALLO companion identity and Korean copy. The user rejected the first large cream/mint UI; use a fresh natural woodland/wood/earth palette and compact game HUD. Prioritize Ninja Voltage village UX references, with CookieRun and Slime kingdom-building references. Reuse project assets without importing those games’ IP artwork.
